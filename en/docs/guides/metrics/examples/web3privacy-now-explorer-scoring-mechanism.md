# Web3Privacy Now — Explorer Scoring Mechanism

**Source:** Web3Privacy Now, "Explorer: Scoring mechanism breakdown," 9 October 2024
**Scope:** Applied to web3 privacy projects in the Privacy Explorer (explorer.web3privacy.info)
**Method note (their words):** projects are ranked on filled-in information "with little to no conditional logic"
**Total possible score:** 150 points

---

## Openness — 55 points

*"Reflects a project's commitment to clear communication and accessibility of information."*

| Criterion               | Points | Rationale                                                    | What to look for                                             |
| ----------------------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Team Information        | 10     | Visible leadership enhances credibility and reduces rug-pull risk | Detailed profiles of core team members on the official website |
| Documentation           | 10     | Comprehensive documentation facilitates understanding and adoption | Well-organised, up-to-date docs, easily accessible online    |
| GitHub Presence         | 10     | Open-source code promotes trust and collaboration            | Active repository with regular updates and community engagement |
| Social Media Engagement | 5      | Active channels indicate community involvement and information flow | Regularly updated Twitter, Discord, or Telegram              |
| Whitepaper Availability | 10     | Outlines vision and technical approach                       | Accessible whitepaper; a gitbook or extensive documentation can substitute |
| Funding Transparency    | 10     | Clear funding information builds investor confidence         | Disclosed funding rounds or sources; "bootstrapped" or "self-funded" is acceptable |

---

## Technology — 60 points

*"Assesses the project's technical foundation and security measures."*

| Criterion                      | Points | Rationale                                                   | What to look for                                          |
| ------------------------------ | ------ | ----------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Mainnet Deployment             | 10     | A live mainnet demonstrates a functional product            | Confirmation of deployment on a main blockchain network   |
| Open Source Code               | 20     | Allows community review and contribution                    | Publicly available code under an open-source licence      |
| Non-Custodial Asset Management | 10     | Gives users control over their assets                       | Architecture letting users retain control of private keys |
| Immutable Smart Contracts      | 10     | Non-upgradeable contracts reduce risk of unexpected changes | Contracts deployed without upgradeable features           |
| Security Audits                | 10     | Third-party audits identify and address vulnerabilities     | Published results from third-party security firms         |

---

## Privacy — 35 points

*"Evaluates how well a project safeguards user information and autonomy."*

| Criterion                | Points | Rationale                                        | What to look for                                        |
| ------------------------ | ------ | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Privacy Policy           | 10     | A clear policy informs users about data handling | Comprehensive, easily accessible privacy policy         |
| Minimal KYC Requirements | 10     | Limited data collection respects user privacy    | Systems designed to operate without mandatory KYC       |
| Regulatory Compliance    | 5      | Demonstrates commitment to legal operation       | Clear communication about regulatory status and efforts |
| Default Privacy Settings | 10     | Privacy by default protects users proactively    | Strong privacy features implemented as the default      |

---

## Totals

| Category   | Points  |
| ---------- | ------- |
| Openness   | 55      |
| Technology | 60      |
| Privacy    | 35      |
| **Total**  | **150** |

---

## Notes

- Scores are **summed** into a single ranking figure.
- The authors explicitly invite feedback and criticism, and describe the model as under active revision.
- No accessibility, environmental, exit/portability, or dark-pattern criteria appear in this model.
