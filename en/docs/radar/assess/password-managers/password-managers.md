### The Canadian angle

**1Password is the standout Canadian option.** It was founded in Toronto and is developed by the Canadian company AgileBits/1Password. Its current individual price is about **US$2.99/month when paid annually**. 

**Bitwarden** is particularly interesting if you care about open source and control. Its server is open source under AGPL, it offers cloud hosting, and it can also be self-hosted. The current individual premium price is **US$19.80/year**, while the free version is unusually capable. 

**Proton Pass** is worth considering if privacy and open-source software are priorities. Proton is Swiss rather than Canadian, but Pass has open-source clients and supports passkeys, aliases and 2FA secrets.
