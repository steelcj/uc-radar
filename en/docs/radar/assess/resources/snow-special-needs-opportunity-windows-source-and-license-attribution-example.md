## Regarding Attributions



1. **Include the article title** (and author, if one is identified). CC BY attribution is strongest when the original work is clearly identified.
2. **State whether you've modified the work.** If you've only reformatted it for your website, say so. If it's an exact copy, say "This document is republished without modification."

I'd recommend something like this:

```markdown
## License and Source

This document was originally published by **SNOW (Special Needs Opportunity Windows)**, an initiative of the **Inclusive Design Research Centre (IDRC)** at **OCAD University**.

**Original article:** *Article Title*  
https://snow.idrc.ocadu.ca/articles/...

**License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)  
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

This edition has been reformatted for publication on the Universal Cake website. No substantive changes have been made to the original content.
```

Or, if you are publishing the article exactly as written:

```markdown
## License and Source

This document was originally published by **SNOW (Special Needs Opportunity Windows)**, an initiative of the **Inclusive Design Research Centre (IDRC)** at **OCAD University**.

**Original article:** *Article Title*  
https://snow.idrc.ocadu.ca/articles/...

**License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)  
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

This document is republished without modification under the terms of the CC BY 4.0 license.
```

I also like your heading, **"License and Source"**. It's concise, clear, and immediately tells readers both where the content came from and under what terms it is being shared. It's a good convention to adopt consistently across your Universal Cake publications.
